Southern Hemisphere Seasons - Configuration Guide
=================================================

config.json settings:
---------------------

"UseCustomWeather": (boolean)
  - true: Use the custom weather settings from customWeather.json
  - false: Use SPT's default weather settings

"ForceSeason": (number or null)
  - null: Automatic - follows Southern Hemisphere calendar:
    * Summer: December 1 to February 28
    * Autumn: March 1 to April 30  
    * Late Autumn: May 1 to May 31
    * Winter: June 1 to August 31
    * Early Spring: September 1 to September 30
    * Spring: October 1 to November 30

  Force Season values:
  - 0: Summer
  - 1: Autumn
  - 2: Spring
  - 3: Storm
  - 4: Late Autumn
  - 5: Early Spring
  - 6: Winter

Example configurations:
-----------------------

Automatic Southern Hemisphere seasons with custom weather:
{
  "UseCustomWeather": true,
  "ForceSeason": null
}

Force Winter season with default SPT weather:
{
  "UseCustomWeather": false,
  "ForceSeason": 6
}

Force Summer with custom weather:
{
  "UseCustomWeather": true,
  "ForceSeason": 0
}

Season Lengths (Fixed - not configurable):
------------------------------------------
- Summer: 90 days (Dec 1 - Feb 28)
- Autumn: 61 days (Mar 1 - Apr 30)
- Late Autumn: 31 days (May 1 - May 31)
- Winter: 92 days (Jun 1 - Aug 31)
- Early Spring: 30 days (Sep 1 - Sep 30)
- Spring: 61 days (Oct 1 - Nov 30)
